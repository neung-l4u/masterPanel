Our story

From Damnoen Saduak to your street.

Our family ran a tiny massage stall beside the canals of Damnoen Saduak — boats drifting past, the smell of coconut and grilled bananas in the air. We brought that easy, friendly feeling here.

No pretension, no rush. Just skilled Thai therapists, fair prices and a room where you can finally switch off.

  • Qualified Thai therapists, many with 10+ years' experience
  • Health fund rebates available for remedial massage
  • Walk-ins welcome — or book online in seconds
