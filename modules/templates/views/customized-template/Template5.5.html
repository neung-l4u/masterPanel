Generational recipes · Local farmers
01

Fresh local flavors, best enjoyed at our table. That line isn't marketing — it's the whole operating manual.

Our recipes came over in notebooks and memories: Tom Yum built on stock that simmers all morning, Pad Thai pulled from the wok at the exact right second, Green Curry pounded from scratch — never from a jar.

The produce doesn't travel far. Australian farmers grow our herbs, chillies and greens, and what arrives in the morning is on plates by evening. Two rooms, two cities, one standard.
