DEMOEATS

DemoEats is a Thai restaurant built on recipes handed down, argued over and perfected across generations. Curry pastes are pounded by hand, stocks simmer all day, and the wok never really cools down.

We buy from local farmers because lemongrass, chili and basil should taste loud. Two kitchens, one standard — Brisbane and Melbourne — with contemporary dining rooms that stay true to their roots.
