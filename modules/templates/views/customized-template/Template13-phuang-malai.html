Our welcome

Strung by hand, one bud at a time

Every morning our team threads fresh jasmine buds, rose petals and marigold into small garlands — the same way Thai families prepare malai for temples, elders and honoured guests.

It sets the tone for everything that follows: patience, respect and attention to the smallest detail. Your massage is made the same way.
