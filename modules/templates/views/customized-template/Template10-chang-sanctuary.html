Our sanctuary

A little piece of Thailand, right here.

DemoSpa was started by two sisters from Chiang Rai who wanted a place that felt like home — warm, welcoming, a bit of laughter at reception, and seriously good massage.

Our therapists are all Thai-trained and certified. We keep prices fair, rooms spotless and the tea always hot.
