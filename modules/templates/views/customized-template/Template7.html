Generational recipes, zero chill

DemoEats started with a hand-written recipe book — splattered, stained, and passed down through three generations of cooks who never once measured a thing.

We pound our curry pastes by hand, buy from local farmers who pick up the phone, and balance every plate across hot, sour, sweet and salty until it sings. Two rooms, one recipe book, no jar sauces — and no apologies for the chili.
