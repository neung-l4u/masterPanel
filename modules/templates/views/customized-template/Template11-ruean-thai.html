The House

A teak house built for stillness

Our spa is modelled on the raised teak houses of central Thailand — high ceilings, open shutters and the scent of jasmine drifting through. Shoes come off at the door. Phones go quiet. Time slows.

Every therapist trained in the Wat Pho tradition and works with the patience it asks for: no rushing, no upselling, only careful hands.

— Khun Somjai, owner & head therapist

The Rite

One stick of incense

A visit follows the length of an incense stick. Watch it burn as you read.

    The Welcomeไหว้

    10 minutes

    A wai at the door, cool pandan water and a quiet chat about where your body is holding tension.

  2. ๒

    The Cleansingล้างเท้า

    10 minutes

    Your feet bathed in warm water with kaffir lime and tamarind, then wrapped in a soft cotton towel.

  3. ๓

  4. ๔
