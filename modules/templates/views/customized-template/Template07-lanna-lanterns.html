Let the night carry your worries away.

Every November in Chiang Mai, thousands of lanterns rise into the sky — each one carrying away a worry. Our massages do the same, one knot at a time.

From the north

Slow hands from the mountains

Northern Thai massage is softer and slower than the Bangkok style — long, rhythmic pressure, gentle stretches and the famous Tok Sen, where a tamarind-wood hammer taps tension out of the muscles.

Our rooms are lit by lanterns, warmed by teak and scented with dried herbs from Chiang Mai. Come in tense. Leave glowing.
