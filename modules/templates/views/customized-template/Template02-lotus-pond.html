Our story

A little pond of calm, right in the city.

Our founder grew up beside her grandmother's lotus pond in Ayutthaya, where neighbours came for herbal oils and gentle hands. DemoSpa is our way of bringing that slow afternoon feeling to you.

Every therapist is trained in traditional Thai technique and blends oils fresh for each guest — nothing pre-mixed, nothing rushed.
