Our Story
01

No jars. No shortcuts. No apologies for the chili.

DemoEats started with a hand-written recipe book — splattered, stained, and passed down through three generations of cooks who never once measured a thing. That book still runs both kitchens.

We pound our curry pastes by hand, buy from local farmers who pick up the phone, and balance every plate across hot, sour, sweet and salty until it sings. Fresh local flavors, best enjoyed at our table.
