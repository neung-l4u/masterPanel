Who we are

fresh, every single day

A thoughtful expression of Thai craft.

DemoEats began with a simple mission: bring the cooking of home into the lives of our friends and neighbours across Australia — generational recipes, made with care, served warm.

The produce doesn't travel far. Local farmers grow our herbs, chillies and greens, and what arrives in the morning is on plates by evening. Two rooms, two cities, one standard.
