03 — The interview

“Pressure is a conversation, not a contest.”

Khun Ploy trained at the Wat Pho school in Bangkok before moving to Australia in 2011. We asked her what most people get wrong about Thai massage.

Does it have to hurt to work? No. The right pressure feels strong but safe — like a good stretch. If you're holding your breath, it's too much. Tell us. We'll adjust.

What should first-timers expect? Loose clothes, a mat or bed, and a lot of movement. We'll fold, rock and stretch you. It's like yoga where someone else does the work.

Your favourite treatment? Traditional Thai, 90 minutes. Sixty is a fix. Ninety is a reset.

“Sixty minutes is a fix. Ninety minutes is a reset.”

1
