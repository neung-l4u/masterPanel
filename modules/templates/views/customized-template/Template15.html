We're Picky

Every curry and stir-fry is built when you order it, so the chili can go from barely-there to properly fierce without touching the rest of the balance.

Most of the list is naturally gluten-free — rice noodles, rice, fresh herbs. Tell your server and we'll steer you clear of the soy.

Curry pastes are pounded in a granite mortar each morning. Stocks simmer all day. There is no shortcut in this kitchen and never has been.

Lemongrass, holy basil, bird's eye chili and galangal arrive from farms we visit ourselves, picked the same morning they hit the wok.

Nothing here is a solo course. Everything lands in the middle of the table, and stays there until it's gone.
