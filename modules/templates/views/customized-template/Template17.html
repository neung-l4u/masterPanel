Our Story

Recipes That Crossed an Ocean

Every curry we serve began in a family kitchen in Thailand, long before it reached Australia. The recipes weren't written down — they were watched, tasted and corrected over generations, then carried here whole.

We still make them the slow way: pastes pounded by hand each morning, stocks that simmer for hours, and nothing — truly nothing — from a jar.

Our Farmers

Two Kitchens, One Standard

Brisbane and Melbourne run on the same rule: if it isn't fresh from a local farmer, it doesn't go in the wok. Herbs, chillies and greens arrive daily, and the menu bends to what the season gives us.

It's why the same dish tastes right in both cities — and why it's best enjoyed at our table, served family style.
