Our Story

Two Kitchens, One Standard

Brisbane and Melbourne run on the same rule: if it isn't fresh from a local farmer, it doesn't go in the wok. Herbs, chillies and greens arrive daily, and the menu bends to what the season gives us.

The recipes weren't written down. They were watched, tasted and corrected across generations in a family kitchen in Thailand, then carried here whole — which is why the same dish tastes right in both cities.

Everything lands in the middle of the table. That's not a serving style; it's the point.
